# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-14)

First release.

- Wild becomes the fifth suit, which counts as every suit. The Lovers, Standard packs, Familiar, Grim and
  Incantation move cards to the Wild suit at their vanilla rates, and a Wild card keeps its enhancement. Suit
  tarots and Sigil can take a card out of it.
- Spectrum, Straight Spectrum, Spectrum House and Spectrum Five, shared through SpectrumAPI, with Quaoar, Haumea,
  Sedna and Makemake. A card fills one suit even when it counts as several, so Smeared Joker works; Four Fingers
  shortens a Spectrum to four cards but never drops the Wild.
- Bulwark, with Asteroid Belt, stepping aside for Cryptid's while that one is on.
- Wacky Joker, Shrewd Joker and The Coalition.
- The Idol, Castle, Driver's License, Smeared Joker's unlock and the Flushed achievement understand the Wild suit.
- A fix for SpectrumAPI registering content under the wrong mod when several mods supply it.
- Art made from Balatro's own, as a mashup of the vanilla suits, with a `*` pip.
