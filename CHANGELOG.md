# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version.

- Wild becomes the fifth suit, which counts as every suit. Everything that gave the Wild enhancement now moves
  cards to the Wild suit, keeping their enhancement.
- Spectrum, Straight Spectrum, Spectrum House and Spectrum Five (through SpectrumAPI), with Quaoar, Haumea, Sedna
  and Makemake.
- Bulwark, with Asteroid Belt (when Cryptid isn't installed).
- Wacky Joker, Shrewd Joker, The Coalition and Envious Joker.
